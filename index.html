The Reserve

Ecology and restorative justice, growing from the same roots

In 2023, the Crecer en Paz Foundation gifted 406 hectares of tropical dry forest to Sembrandopaz, which now stewards the land as Morrocoy Nature Reserve. It sits in El Salado, El Carmen de Bolívar, a community still healing from years of conflict.

Today the reserve is dedicated to conserving and restoring Tropical Dry Forest, one of the most endangered ecosystems on Earth, while giving the surrounding community a stake in what grows back.

One reserve, two plots

The 406 hectares of Morrocoy aren't one unbroken block. The reserve is made up of two adjoining plots, Morrocoy 1 and Morrocoy 2, mapped and monitored across El Salado, El Carmen de Bolívar.

What restoration looks like

From open scar to closed canopy

Both plots were left exposed by deforestation before Sembrandopaz began restoring them. This is the starting point every hectare of today's forest had to grow back from.

Restoring land like this isn't instant. Closed-canopy tropical dry forest takes years of active planting, monitoring, and protection to return. Every gift to Morrocoy funds that work.
